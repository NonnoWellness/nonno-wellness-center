About Nonno Wellness Center

Evidence-based treatment. Care that feels like family.

We're a Redondo Beach addiction treatment center offering Intensive Outpatient Programs and structured sober living for men and women. Whether you're stepping down from residential care or need a flexible program that fits around work and family, we build a plan around cognitive behavioral therapy, individual counseling, and group support — not a one-size-fits-all script.

We're licensed by the California Department of Health Care Services (DHCS Certification #191522AP) and serve clients across the greater Los Angeles area, including San Pedro, Torrance, El Segundo, Hermosa Beach, Manhattan Beach, and Palos Verdes.

Common Questions

FAQs

Do you accept insurance?

We accept most major PPO insurance plans. Send us your insurance card or call us and we'll verify your benefits, usually the same day.

What's the difference between IOP and sober living?

IOP is structured treatment — therapy and counseling sessions several days a week. Sober living is a supportive, accountable home environment. Many clients use both together.

Do you treat both men and women?

Yes. Our IOP and ULSA programs, as well as our sober living support, are open to both men and women. Call us to talk through what fits your situation.

How do I get started?

Call 310-807-1550 or use the contact form below. We'll talk through your situation, verify insurance, and figure out next steps together — no obligation.
