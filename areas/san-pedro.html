Treatment close to home in San Pedro

San Pedro clients have convenient access to our full range of programs, from Intensive Outpatient to structured sober living, all within a short drive. As home to the Port of Los Angeles, San Pedro has a large community of longshoremen, dockworkers, and other ILWU and union members — and our ULSA program was built specifically with that kind of shift-based, physically demanding work in mind.

Whether you're looking for structured Intensive Outpatient treatment, a stable sober living environment, or both, our Redondo Beach campus is built to serve clients from across the South Bay and greater Los Angeles — including San Pedro.

FAQs

Is Nonno Wellness Center convenient from San Pedro?

Yes — our campus is based in Redondo Beach, roughly ~20 min drive from San Pedro, and many of our clients commute from throughout the South Bay for IOP sessions. We also now have a sober living home located directly in San Pedro for ILWU members.

Do you accept insurance for clients outside Redondo Beach?

Yes, your coverage isn't affected by where you live. Use our insurance verification form and we'll check your benefits the same way regardless of your city.

Do you work with longshoremen and ILWU members?

Yes. Our ULSA program is built around port and union shift schedules, with peer support from others who understand the physical demands and unpredictable hours of dock work. We also now operate a sober living home in San Pedro exclusively for ILWU members — call us to talk through availability.
